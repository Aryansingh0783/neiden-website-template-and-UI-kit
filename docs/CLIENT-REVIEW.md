# Client review guide

For the person looking at the new Devbhoomi Realtors website for the first time. It takes about twenty minutes. Open the preview link on your phone and again on a computer.

## What to look at
1. **Home page.** Scroll slowly from the top. Watch the hero, the numbers that count up (25+ and 10+), the three featured plots, the areas, the process, the cottage section and the questions at the end.
2. **All land.** Open Properties. Try the filters (area, road, status, type), the sort menu and the search box. Open two plots, one with a video and one without.
3. **A plot page.** Check the price, the size, the papers line, and the WhatsApp button. Press it: WhatsApp should open with a message about that plot already written.
4. **Areas, Cottages, Services, About, From outside the state.** Read for anything that is wrong or out of date.
5. **Forms.** On "What is your land worth?" fill in the form. The grey box shows the exact message that will go to WhatsApp. Press the button: WhatsApp opens with that message. Try the Referral form and the Contact form too.
6. **Phone.** The black WhatsApp and Call bar sits at the bottom of every page.
7. **Ask us.** The small button at the bottom right opens the assistant. It will say it is not switched on until an AI key is added (see "Still to do").

## Questions for you
1. Do you want the small bracket labels with numbers (such as "[ 01 DEVBHOOMI REALTORS ]") kept? They are part of the design style. They can be removed.
2. Plots with a view of "Ask us" or an aspect of "To confirm" (listed in `docs/QA-REPORT.md`): can you supply the facts?
3. Do you want a better video or a second photograph for the plots? Today each plot has one photograph and the videos are small.
4. Is the wording right everywhere? Anything you would say differently?
5. The private pages `/stats/` and `/leads/` are not in this site. Keep them running on the old system, or rebuild them here?
